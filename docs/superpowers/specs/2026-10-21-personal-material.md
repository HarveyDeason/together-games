# Personal material — the things that make it theirs

Captured 2026-10-21. This is the raw material Harvey supplied for personalising
any game on the site. It is the scarcest input in the project: everything else
can be re-derived from code, this cannot.

## People

- **Harvey** — Keynsham, Bristol, UK.
- **Shruti** — Nagpur, Maharashtra, India.

## Places

- **Keynsham / Bristol** — home end. Clifton Suspension Bridge, the balloons.
- **Nagpur** — the Orange City; Zero Mile Stone (the geographic centre of India);
  tiger country; saoji food. Biryani is the agreed destination.
- **Cappadocia, Turkey** — the fairy chimneys and the hot air balloons. A place
  that means something to the two of them. Note: Bristol has balloons too, so
  there is a real visual rhyme between the home end and Cappadocia worth using.

## Disasters (the things that set you back)

- **Wifi down / no internet.**
- **Shruti asleep, didn't answer the call.** The most personal of the set, and
  the most useful: it is specific, it is funny, and it is about the two of them
  rather than about travel.

## Phrases

- "Lovely jubbly"
- "Darling"
- "Madame"
- "Jiggly Boo"

## How to use these

Jokes about airline food are generic and will not land. These will. Favour the
specific ones (Shruti asleep, Jiggly Boo) over the general ones (wifi down) when
there is only room for a few. Phrases work best as the text a square says when
you land on it, not as square names.
